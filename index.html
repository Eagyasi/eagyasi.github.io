Akwaaba /ɑ́kʷàːbá/ ‘Welcome’

Emmanuel Appiah Gyasi /ɪˈmæn.jʊəl ɑpʲɪa d͡ʒɛsɪ/

PhD Student in Linguistics · University of Wisconsin, Milwaukee

I am a budding Linguist, Educator, and Community Enthusiast. My research spans laboratory phonology, psycholinguistics, and second language linguistics, with a current focus on complex segments such as Ewe's labiovelars [kp, gb] and affricates [ts, dz], studied through theoretical and experimental methods.

I am also an instructor of record at UWM and a co-organizer of the Phonetics & Phonology Reading Group (P-Group).
